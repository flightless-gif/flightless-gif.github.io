You don't know how much time has passed when you come back to yourself. It takes days? weeks? before your perception of reality feels stable again.

You weren't able to turn the project in on time. You got it in late, but you don't feel better. You're struggling to stay on top of things anyways; you were too out of it to do much work for enough time to get a backlog of tasks.

But you've done this before. And even though the toll the stress took on your mind and body is still present, it's mark left right beside all the past times, you know you'll have to do this again. And yet, with every repeat, you learn what to do and what not to do. You didn't do well this time; maybe in the future, you'll be able to keep more of yourself.

You lost yourself. Would you like to try a different way?
